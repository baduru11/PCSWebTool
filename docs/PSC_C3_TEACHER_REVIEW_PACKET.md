Full-bank adjudication of all 104 production C3 items against the exam's own
scoring bases, following the teacher's requirement that every item match the

**Adjudication basis (in priority order):**
1. 《普通话水平测试用普通话常见量词、名词搭配表》 — the official appendix that
   keys the 量词 sub-section, including its parenthetical cross-listings

Every decision below was cross-checked by three independent adversarial review

carry `accepted_answers_review.status = school_teacher_review_pending` in the
database — **your review clears or reverses them**.

Section structure verified against HKUST CLE's published rules
(<https://cle.hkust.edu.hk/tests/psc/psc/rules>): 词语判断 10 组（−0.25）＋

## 3. Two-standard-forms sentence items now accepting both — 13 items

Real 语序判断 items contrast a Putonghua form with a dialect form. These items
each contained **two** standard forms; both are now accepted so a learner
choosing either is not marked wrong:

发给你邮件↔发邮件给你 · 来没来↔有没有来（方言形式是「有来没有」）·
洗澡了没有↔洗了澡没有 · 听不听得懂↔听得懂吗 · 几点↔几点钟 ·
真可爱↔可爱得很 · 能帮我吗↔可不可以帮我 · 比他大三岁↔大他三岁 ·
不够咸↔太淡了 · 出太阳了↔天晴了 · 常常↔时不时 · 把窗户打开↔打开窗户 ·
我提醒你一事↔我提醒你一件事（school key 一事 kept as primary; 一件事 is the
spoken-register form — consider re-keying）

Not accepted after adversarial review: 这双鞋**好贵**（好＋形 is the 港式
calque this item is designed to penalize — kept wrong; flag if you disagree）,
他**在上海住**（incomplete as a bare declarative）.

## 5. 词语判断 items pairing two Putonghua words (2 fixed, 1 open)

- 土豆 vs **马铃薯**: both standard (马铃薯 is the formal name; the official
  量词表 itself lists 土豆 as a Putonghua noun) → both accepted.
- 聊天 vs **聊天儿**: same word, erhua variant → both accepted.
- **Open question for you**: 妻子 vs 老婆. 现汉 labels 老婆 as 〈口〉(standard
  colloquial), not 〈方〉. Whether 老婆 appears in the 方言 column of the
  official 《普通话与方言词语对照表》 decides this item; we could not obtain
  that table. Currently 老婆 is still marked wrong. Related: 媳妇 (standard for
  "son's wife") sits in the same option set.
